Add a rough notebook for free typing

A third tab beside the procedure and the notebook, with a plain ruled
textarea for working things out: what a result rules out, which groups are
gone, anything worth remembering. It saves as you type and survives a
reload, unlike the structured notebook, since typed notes are the one thing
that must not disappear. Printing the record appends the rough work at the
end.
